# Privacy Policy for Buaic: Workout Tracker

**Last Updated:** November 14, 2026 (Buaic 2.0)

Nathan Doyle ("Developer", "we", "us", or "our") built the **Buaic: Workout Tracker** mobile application as a standalone, offline-first fitness utility. This Privacy Policy explains what happens to the information you enter in the app.

**In short: Buaic does not collect any data. Your workouts stay on your device, and the app never sends them anywhere.** There is no account, no advertising and no analytics. The App Store privacy label for Buaic is "Data Not Collected".

---

## 1. Data Collection and Local Storage
**We do not collect, transmit, store on external servers, sell, or share any personal data or usage information.**

Everything you enter in Buaic (routines, exercises, sets, weights and repetitions, notes, personal records, workout history and settings) is stored only on your device, in an on-device SQLite database. Buaic does not send it to us or to anyone else.

Buaic keeps a few safety copies of this data, also only on your device:
* When you update from Buaic 1.0, a copy of your 1.0 data is saved before it is converted to the new format.
* Before you import a backup or restore a copy, a copy of your current data is saved so the change can be undone. You can delete these copies in Settings → Data.

If you uninstall the application, all of this data is permanently removed with it.

## 2. Files You Export
Buaic creates files only when you ask it to:
* **Backup (JSON)** and **sets export (CSV)** from Settings → Data.
* **Workout share card** (an image) from the workout summary.

The app opens your operating system's share menu, and you choose where the file goes (for example Files, a cloud drive, email or a messaging app). Whatever you choose then handles the file under its own terms; Buaic does not upload it anywhere. A temporary copy of the latest export stays in the app's own storage until the next export or until the system clears it.

## 3. Device Backups
If your phone's own backup is turned on (for example iCloud Backup on iPhone, or Android's device backup), it may include Buaic's data, as it does for other apps. That backup is run by Apple or Google under your device and account settings, not by Buaic. Buaic itself never sends your data anywhere.

## 4. Device Permissions and Features
Buaic may ask for, or use, the following on your device:
* **Notifications:** asked the first time a rest timer starts, and used only to alert you on the device when the rest is over. These are local notifications scheduled by the app; no notification data leaves your device. On Android, the alarm permissions let the alert arrive on time.
* **Photos (iPhone, add only):** asked only if you choose "Save Image" for a share card. Buaic can add that image to your library; it cannot see your photos.
* **Home-screen widget and Live Activity:** the widget (iPhone and Android) and the rest-timer Live Activity (iPhone) show a summary of your training. Buaic updates them directly on your device; the information shown is not sent to any server.
* **Haptics and keeping the screen awake** during a workout, both on the device and adjustable in Settings.
* **Rating prompt:** after several finished workouts Buaic may show the system's rating dialog, and on iPhone, Settings → Rate Buaic opens its App Store page. Ratings and reviews are handled by Apple or Google; Buaic does not see them.

## 5. Third-Party Services & Analytics
Buaic does not integrate any third-party analytics SDKs, advertising networks, crash reporters, cloud databases, or user tracking frameworks, and it makes no network requests of its own.

## 6. Children's Privacy
Because Buaic does not collect any personal information from any user, we do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.
