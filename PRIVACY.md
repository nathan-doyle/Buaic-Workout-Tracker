# Privacy Policy for Buaic: Workout Tracker

**Last Updated:** October 20, 2026

Nathan Doyle ("Developer", "we", "us", or "our") built the **Buaic: Workout Tracker** mobile application as a standalone, offline-first fitness utility. This Privacy Policy informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our application.

---

## 1. Data Collection and Local Storage
**We do not collect, transmit, store on external servers, sell, or share any personal data or usage information.**

All data generated within Buaic—including custom workout routines, exercise names, weight and repetition logs, personal records (PRs), and session history—is stored exclusively on your local device within an on-device SQLite database. If you uninstall the application, all locally stored data is permanently removed alongside the application.

## 2. Device Permissions
To provide core workout tracking functionality, Buaic may request access to the following local device capabilities:
* **Local Notifications & Alarms:** Used strictly on-device to schedule and sound alerts when your workout rest countdown timer finishes. No notification data leaves your device.
* **Image Export & System Share Sheet:** When you choose to export or share a workout summary recap card, the application generates a temporary local image snapshot on your device and opens your operating system's native share menu. We cannot access your photos, contacts, or shared messages.

## 3. Third-Party Services & Analytics
Buaic does not integrate any third-party analytics SDKs, advertising networks, cloud databases, or user tracking frameworks.

## 4. Children's Privacy
Because Buaic does not collect any personal information from any user, we do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
