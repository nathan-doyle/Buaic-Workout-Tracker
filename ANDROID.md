# Buaic on Android: become a tester

Buaic is in a closed test on Google Play before it goes public. Testers get the full app now, free, with no account, no ads and no tracking.

You need an Android phone and the Google account you use for the Play Store.

## Sign up (2 minutes)

1. **Join the tester group.** Open **[groups.google.com/g/buaic-testers](https://groups.google.com/g/buaic-testers)** and tap **Join group**. Use the same Google account as the Play Store on your phone.
2. **Opt in.** Open **[play.google.com/apps/testing/com.buaic](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.buaic)** and tap **Become a tester**.
3. **Install.** Tap the Google Play link on that page, or search the Play Store for **Buaic**. It can take a few minutes to show up after step 2.

## Please stay for 14 days

Google lets a new app go public only after at least 12 testers have stayed in its test for 14 days in a row. Keep Buaic installed and log a workout or two. You can leave any time after that, or stay and get updates first.

## Feedback

Found a bug or missing something? Send private feedback from Buaic's page in the Play Store. Only the developer sees it.

## Privacy

The app collects nothing: your workouts stay on your phone (see the [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md)). Joining the group shows your Google account's email address to the developer, who uses it only to run the test. Other members can't see it.

To leave: open the opt-in link above and tap **Leave the program**, then leave the group.
